# Experiments

## object_interaction_kinematics

This experiment checks whether retargeted motion can maintain hand contact with a carried box while avoiding object and ground penetration. It evaluates the paper's claim that interaction-aware retargeting preserves contact without creating physical artifacts.

## terrain_interaction_kinematics

This experiment checks whether retargeted motion can traverse a step-like terrain while keeping stance feet and support hands stable. It evaluates the paper's claim that terrain interaction can be preserved while enforcing hard kinematic feasibility.
