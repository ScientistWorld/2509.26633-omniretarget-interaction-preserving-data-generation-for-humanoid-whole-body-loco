# Reproduction details

The final gym implements the kinematic interaction-preserving retargeting procedure in Sections III and Appendix D of `paper/2509.26633.md`. The earlier planar synthetic reproduction is superseded; its historical results remain only in progress/audit records.

`method/omniretarget.py` constructs 3D Delaunay interaction meshes from 15 human/robot anatomical correspondences and 100 deterministically sampled scene points. The uniformly weighted Laplacian deformation energy preserves body/object/terrain relationships. Objects use local coordinates; human scale maps to the 1.32 m robot by height ratio, while the physical carried box retains its size.

Each frame warm-starts from the previous configuration. Sequential SOCP steps minimize linearized mesh deformation plus temporal smoothing while enforcing linearized scene clearance, joint positions and velocities, and stance-foot equality. The trust radius is 0.2, with at most 10 iterations per ordinary frame and 50 initially. Initial infeasibility retries without only the trust-region bound, as in the authors' release; no soft physical-constraint fallback is used. Stance uses the paper's 1 cm/s horizontal source-foot speed. Quaternion increments use MuJoCo's tangent-space kinematics. Clarabel solves the conic subproblems. Collision queries explicitly restore witness ordering in the pinned MuJoCo version.

Augmented instances first solve their nominal scene. Modified-object retargeting then uses object-local meshes, the paper's lower-body anchoring cost and initial-foot constraints. Terrain augmentation changes platform height and uses a fixed ground grid in the mesh. Reference defaults have no additional contact/support task costs; those are honest improvement probes in `method/audit_variants.py`. The independent comparator in `baseline/keypoint_ik.py` uses keypoint IK rather than the mesh. It is not a reproduction of PHC, GMR or VideoMimic.

Data are public OMOMO large-box clips from the processed release used by the authors, plus the authors' released climbing motion capture. Public subjects 3/8/10/12 and climbing recording 1 are separated from held-out subjects 7/16 and recordings 0/2/3. Box motion windows extend to 600 frames at 30 Hz. Test scores 99 nominal object clips, 14 terrain windows and 212 modified-scene inputs. Train has 44 inputs at comparable difficulty. Private construction, raw data and diagnostics stay in `eval/test/`; public downloads retain train data only.

The robot is the authors' public Unitree G1 with 29 joints and floating base (36 q coordinates). Scene collision hulls are reduced deterministically to at most 128 vertices. `scripts/download.sh` fetches all public source assets from pinned/public URLs; the generated private corpus is tracked with provenance under `eval/test/data/`. Dependencies and container build instructions are in `environment/`.

Evaluation accepts only complete raw q trajectories through arbitrary `scripts/retargeters/*.sh` registrations. It independently reconstructs robot geometry and measures contact, penetration, skating, support failure, unwanted contact, pelvis path and body-pose error. Impossible or failed runs receive all worst values. Ground truth, thresholds, comparisons and test size belong to the evaluator. Both no-argument split entrypoints score every registration and write under each experiment's `results`. Rank directions, coefficient magnitudes and across-attempt population-standard-deviation normalization are described in `briefing/evaluation.md` and `scoring/reference.json`.

This is a partial reproduction of the 3D kinematic claim, not exact Table II quality. MuJoCo/Clarabel replace Drake, the corpus is a subset, annotations are geometric, and collision hulls are reduced. The reference still has substantial nonlinear penetration and contact loss. Self-collision, dynamics, downstream RL, hardware and full cross-embodiment studies are not reproduced. Paper-reported values are retained unchanged as underscore metadata, separate from measured gym results. `HARDENING.md` gives final results, headroom, shortcut replays and unresolved limitations.

Run `bash scripts/download.sh` with internet before compute; then `bash scripts/run.sh` reproduces the reference and keypoint baseline on train and hidden test and validates the workspace. Scientific execution belongs in a submitted compute job. The default job script runs reference reproduction; the private audit workflow is available separately as `python -m eval.test.audit`.
